Our Story

INDIGICUL was founded on one belief: leather should be made with patience. We reject the quick, disposable goods sold on the sidewalk and make leather goods that last for decades.

We work with crocodile, snake and ostrich leather, and finish each piece with solid brass, bronze or silver accessories. Every product is checked by hand before it reaches you.
